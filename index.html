Marvin

Marvin is a personal assistant that one person built for their own use. It isn't a product or a service. No one else can use it or sign in to it.

What it does

  • It reads its owner's Gmail, to sort and summarise new mail.
  • It reads its owner's Google Calendar events, and adds or changes events when its owner asks.
  • It sends an e-mail from its owner's account only after the owner has approved that exact message.

It runs on its owner's own server and reports to its owner through a private Telegram bot.

Privacy

How Marvin handles Google data is set out in its privacy policy.
