Marvin privacy policy

Last changed 18 November 2026

Who this covers

Marvin has one user: the person who built it, its owner. It isn't offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it. This page explains what Marvin does with its owner's Google data.

What Marvin can access

  • Gmail, read only (gmail.readonly): to read and sort the owner's mail. This permission can't send, delete or change any mail.
  • Gmail, send only (gmail.send): to send messages the owner has approved. This permission can't read any mail. It's held by a separate program from the one that reads mail.
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events): to read the owner's events, and to add or change events when the owner asks.

How it's used

Only to give the owner summaries, reminders and drafts, and to carry out what the owner approves. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to build a profile of anyone.

Who else handles it

Marvin is run with these services, and data passes through them only to do the jobs above:

  • Anthropic: parts of a message may be sent to Anthropic's Claude model, through the owner's own Claude subscription, to summarise it or draft a reply. Anthropic handles it under its own terms.
  • Telegram: summaries and drafts reach the owner through a private Telegram bot.
  • Oracle Cloud: hosts the owner's server, where Marvin runs.
  • GitHub: holds the owner's notes, in a private repository.

No one else receives it.

Where it's kept, and for how long

On the owner's own server and in the owner's private notes, only as long as the owner wants it. Records of conversations with Marvin are deleted after 90 days unless the owner keeps one. Google's sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the server.

Withdrawing access

Marvin's access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Third-party connections page: choose Marvin, then Delete all connections.

Changes

Any change to this policy is shown by the date at the top of this page.
